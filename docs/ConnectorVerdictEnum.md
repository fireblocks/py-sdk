# ConnectorVerdictEnum

The connector's own verdict for a step, before the step's outcome rules are applied. `null` on a step that did not reach the provider.

## Enum

* `ACCEPT` (value: `'ACCEPT'`)

* `REJECT` (value: `'REJECT'`)

* `ALERT` (value: `'ALERT'`)
