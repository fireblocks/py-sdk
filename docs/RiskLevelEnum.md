# RiskLevelEnum

The provider's risk assessment for a step, normalised across connectors. `null` on a step that did not reach the provider — the absence of a risk level is not the same as low risk.

## Enum

* `SEVERE` (value: `'SEVERE'`)

* `HIGH` (value: `'HIGH'`)

* `MEDIUM` (value: `'MEDIUM'`)

* `LOW` (value: `'LOW'`)

* `UNKNOWN` (value: `'UNKNOWN'`)
